Side effects and who should skip it

Natural does not mean risk-free. Please read this part carefully.

Possible effects include a warm, tingling flush from niacin, stomach upset, headache and lightheadedness. Beet nitrate can slightly lower blood pressure, and ginkgo and dong quai may raise bleeding risk.

Speak to a doctor before use if you:

  • Take blood pressure medicine, nitrate drugs or ED medication such as sildenafil or tadalafil
  • Take blood thinners or have a bleeding disorder
  • Have heart disease or kidney problems
  • Are pregnant, breastfeeding or under 18

Ongoing low stamina or erection problems can point to an underlying health issue that a supplement will not fix. A check-up is worth more than any powder.
